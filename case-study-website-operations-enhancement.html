Website Operations Enhancement Through QA Framework and Structured Onboarding

aCXend strengthened an established DUDA website production process — layering in quality assurance standards, structured onboarding, and continuous improvement to move a digital agency from delivering websites to consistently delivering quality.

80% Fewer post-launch errors
55% Less QA-related rework
Consistent Reporting and visibility
Unified Onboarding and training
Improved Quality and delivery reliability
The Problem

An existing process — but with gaps.

A digital agency with an established website development process on DUDA had already built a steady pipeline of client projects. While their workflow enabled consistent output, underlying inefficiencies began to surface as volume increased.

Common issues included broken links, duplicate content blocks, inconsistent meta tags, and minor formatting errors — problems often discovered late in the process or even after deployment. These led to rework, delays, and occasional client feedback that impacted overall confidence in delivery quality.

At the same time, onboarding new team members proved challenging. Training lacked structure, knowledge transfer varied by trainer, and new hires required extended ramp-up time before reaching full productivity — increasing the likelihood of technical errors and inconsistencies across builds.

Although the agency had a working process, it lacked the quality controls, structured onboarding, and performance visibility needed to scale effectively.
The Approach

Embedding QA, structure, and continuous improvement.

aCXend approached the engagement not by replacing the existing workflow, but by enhancing it — introducing structured layers of quality assurance, reporting, and operational discipline. The first step was designing a comprehensive QA framework tailored to DUDA website builds, with clearly defined checkpoints across the production lifecycle, from initial setup to pre-launch validation.

To reinforce accountability, aCXend introduced structured QA touchpoints — ensuring every website passes through standardized validation stages before moving forward, significantly reducing the risk of errors reaching the final output. In parallel, consistent reporting gave teams visibility into common error types, QA pass rates, rework frequency, and overall build quality.

Together, these layers targeted the critical quality elements behind reliable delivery:

  • Defined QA checkpoints across the lifecycle — link functionality, content duplication, SEO tags, responsiveness, and formatting consistency
  • Consistent reporting on error types, QA pass rates, and rework frequency for continuous, data-informed improvement
  • A structured onboarding framework — step-by-step build guidelines, QA checklists, documented best practices, and hands-on DUDA training modules

The standardized onboarding ensured every new hire follows the same process, understands quality expectations from day one, and contributes faster with reduced risk of errors. Beyond implementation, aCXend continuously challenged the client's existing norms — simplifying steps, eliminating redundancies, and sharing refined best practices — creating a culture of continuous improvement rather than static execution.

The Outcome

Higher-quality builds, faster ramp-up, stronger operations.

By embedding QA frameworks and structured onboarding into the existing process, the agency significantly improved both quality and efficiency — evolving from a process that simply delivered websites into one that consistently delivers quality.

80% reduction in post-launch errors such as broken links and duplicate content
55% decrease in QA-related rework and corrections
Consistent adherence to SEO standards including meta tags and structure
Faster onboarding and improved readiness of new team members
Reduced variability in build quality across projects
Improved visibility through structured reporting and performance tracking

Through QA framework design, onboarding standardization, and operational refinement, aCXend added a critical layer of value — transforming reliability, improving team performance, and ensuring every website meets a higher standard before it goes live.
