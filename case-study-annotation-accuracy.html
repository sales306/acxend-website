100% Annotation Accuracy Through Multi-Layer Trust & Safety Operations

aCXend helped a leading entertainment company move from inconsistent, backlogged content review to a fully governed, audit-ready annotation operation that scales without sacrificing accuracy.

100% Validated Annotation Accuracy
99.8% Talent Protection Compliance
95% Fewer Labeling Defects
3x Content Processing Capacity
100% SLA Compliance Across Operations
The Problem

Growth outpaced the ability to review it accurately.

A leading entertainment company manages a rapidly growing portfolio of digital content featuring performers, creators, and talent distributed across multiple platforms and formats.

Every video required detailed frame-by-frame analysis to identify, classify, and label elements that fed directly into content moderation, trust and safety programs, and AI-powered content management systems — meaning accuracy wasn't optional, it was foundational to talent protection and brand safety.

As production accelerated, internal teams faced mounting backlogs, limited capacity to scale review operations, and rising risk of inconsistent decisions or undetected guideline violations, all while demand for high-quality AI training data kept climbing.

Incorrect labels could quietly undermine talent protection, content governance, brand reputation, and AI model performance all at once — the client needed a partner that could scale review capacity without ever trading away precision.
The Approach

A quality framework built on prevention, not correction.

The engagement began with an extensive review of the client's content moderation policies, talent protection requirements, annotation guidelines, escalation procedures, and classification standards — then a structured certification program to ensure every content specialist demonstrated proficiency before touching production content.

From there, dedicated specialists conducted frame-by-frame video annotation against the client's taxonomy and governance framework, feeding into a rigorous multi-layer human quality review process designed to catch errors before they ever reached delivery.

That review process was built around four distinct layers of scrutiny:

  • Primary annotation by certified specialists, with independent quality validation by dedicated Quality Analysts for every completed task.
  • Expert escalation for complex, ambiguous, or high-risk content, ensuring final policy review by senior quality specialists.
  • Continuous audit and calibration through targeted reviews and quality scorecards to maintain consistency at scale.

Underpinning all four layers were live quality dashboards tracking annotation accuracy, reviewer performance, escalation trends, and SLA adherence — giving both aCXend and the client full visibility to catch and correct drift before it ever reached scale.

The Outcome

100% accuracy, delivered at 3x the scale.

aCXend's Trust & Safety and Data Quality framework enabled the organization to scale content moderation and data annotation while achieving both high, fully validated accuracy and significantly higher throughput.

100% validated annotation accuracy through multi-layer human review
99.8% compliance with content moderation and talent protection standards
95% reduction in labeling defects compared to baseline operations
100% SLA compliance across all content moderation and annotation activity
3x increase in content processing capacity without compromising quality
90% reduction in quality-related escalations and rework requirements
100% successful completion of client quality audits
Higher-quality training datasets powering the client's AI and machine learning initiatives

The engagement left the organization with more than a content moderation program — a durable Trust & Safety and AI Data Quality strategy that protects talent, strengthens governance, and gives every downstream AI initiative a foundation of clean, audit-ready data to build on.
